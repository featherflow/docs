## What's new

Bitscale's LinkedIn Sales Navigator integration has been completely revamped.

Previously (and still true for most competitors), you could only pull in **public filters** from a Sales Nav search URL, things like geography, industry, title, seniority, and company size.

Now, by connecting your own Sales Navigator account, you can also pull in:

- **Saved searches** — any saved search you've already built, with its full list of leads or accounts
- **Account-specific filters** — filters tied to your personal seat, like **connection tier** and **exclusion lists**

If you just need a quick, public-filter search, you can still use a Sales Nav URL directly without connecting an account. See [Import Sales Navigator Leads Using a Search URL](/list/import-sales-nav-leads) for that lighter-weight path.

## Recommended setup: one connection per SDR

We strongly recommend maintaining **one LinkedIn Sales Navigator connection per SDR or GTM engineer** on your team. Each person should connect their own Sales Navigator instance so they can scrape and maintain their own personal lists, rather than sharing a single shared login.

## Step 1: Connect your Sales Navigator account

1. In Bitscale, go to **Integrations**
2. Click **Add Integration**
3. Search for **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** (or find it in the integrations list)
4. Click **Connect LinkedIn**
5. Enter your LinkedIn credentials and log in

Once connected, this account will show up as an available integration instance whenever you set up a new Sales Navigator source.

## Step 2: Get a saved search URL from Sales Navigator

1. In LinkedIn Sales Navigator, open **Saved Searches**
2. Open the saved search you want to pull in
3. Copy the URL from your browser's address bar

This works for saved searches that include account-specific filters (connection tier, exclusions, and so on), not just public filters.

## Step 3: Import into Bitscale

1. From the Bitscale homepage, click **New Grid**
2. Choose **Find People** (or **Find Companies**) using the **Sales Navigator URL** source
3. Select the specific Sales Navigator account/integration you connected in Step 1
4. Paste your saved search URL
5. Click **Import into a new grid**

The pull will show a **Processing** tag while it runs. Depending on the size of the batch, it can take anywhere from a few minutes up to 10-15 minutes to complete.

## What to do next

Once your leads or companies are in the grid, you can:

- enrich emails and phone numbers
- run research enrichments
- qualify against your ICP
- push to your CRM or outbound tools
